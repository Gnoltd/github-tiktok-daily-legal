## 1. What the application does

The application is a personal automation tool. Once a day it builds a short news video that summarizes public GitHub trending repositories, and uploads that video as a draft to the Operator's own TikTok account through TikTok's Content Posting API. The Operator then reviews the draft and decides whether to publish it.
